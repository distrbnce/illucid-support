Privacy Policy

Your experience stays yours.

Effective July 18, 2026

Overview

Illucid is designed to create immersive visuals without a developer-operated account or backend service. We do not sell personal information, serve advertisements, or use third-party analytics or cross-app tracking.

Camera, microphone, and motion

With your permission, Illucid uses camera input to generate live visuals, microphone input to make supported visuals react to sound, and motion data to make supported visuals respond to device movement. This input is processed on your device for these features. Illucid does not record, store, or transmit camera images, video, audio, or motion data to us.

Information stored on your device

Illucid may store app preferences—such as liked visuals and the current visual configuration—locally on your device. You can remove this information by deleting the app.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple may process purchase and account information under its own privacy policy. Illucid checks purchase status to unlock features and restore purchases.

Children’s privacy

Illucid does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users through a developer-operated service.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if Illucid’s features or privacy practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.

Contact

Questions about this policy or Illucid can be submitted through the public Illucid support-request page. Please do not include personal, payment, or other sensitive information in a public request.
